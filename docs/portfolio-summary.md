# Wealthya: a short case study

## Challenge

Portfolio decisions depend on records, market context, and questions that deserve investigation. When these sit in separate places, the owner has to reconstruct the whole picture before making a decision.

## Approach

I framed the task as a product workflow: organize the portfolio, bring relevant context into view, surface questions for research, and keep final judgment with the human owner. I used local-first design and privacy as constraints from the start.

## My Role

I owned problem framing, requirements, workflow and system design, prioritization, and product decisions. I translated business needs into behavior for the product, coordinated AI-assisted implementation, reviewed outputs, and guided tests and iteration. This is an AI-assisted product development case, not a claim that I personally wrote all code.

## What Was Built

The private Wealthya / OwnerOS project contains a working web application for asset intelligence and decision support, plus iOS work. Its web stack includes Next.js, React, TypeScript, Drizzle ORM, and Cloudflare/vinext. The product remains an evolving private project; this repository explains it without publishing the application or private data.

## How AI Was Used

AI helped accelerate implementation and iteration after the problem and expected behavior were specified. It was used as a collaborator for execution, while product priorities and acceptance remained human decisions.

## How Outputs Were Reviewed

I reviewed generated work against requirements, checked for unsupported assumptions, ran relevant tests, and revised the product when gaps appeared. I treat passing local checks as evidence for those checks, not proof of all user-facing or production behavior.

## Technical Exposure

The project required learning across web interface work, application workflows, data modeling, and iOS development. I worked with technical specialists and AI tools while maintaining a clear view of user needs and system boundaries.

## Outcome / Learning Demonstrated

The result is a real, evolving software product and a repeatable way to move from a business problem to tested software. This demonstrates a practical contribution to team projects at the intersection of AI Business & Product and AI Applications. No user counts, revenue, investment performance, or other unverified outcome is claimed.
