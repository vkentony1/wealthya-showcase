# Privacy and safety by design

This showcase is deliberately separate from Wealthya's private repository. It presents the product problem, a high-level workflow, a conceptual architecture, and a fully synthetic example. It does not distribute the application, operating configuration, private records, or internal instructions.

That separation is an engineering choice: an admissions reviewer can evaluate the candidate's product and systems thinking without receiving sensitive financial data or access to a private runtime. The example record is invented for this portfolio and cannot be used to infer a real portfolio.

The product supports human review and decision making. It does not place trades or execute transactions. The public case study makes no claim of a public production deployment, audited security, commercial traction, or measured financial outcome.
